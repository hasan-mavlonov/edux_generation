<problem>Mantiqiy o'zgaruvchilar $A, B, C$ uchun $F(A, B, C) = (A \text{ YOKI } \text{EMON } B) \text{ VA } (B \text{ YOKI } C)$ mantiqiy ifoda berilgan. $A, B, C$ o'zgaruvchilarning 8 ta mumkin bo me'yoriy qiymat to'plamidan nechtasida $F$ ning qiymati 1 (rost) ga teng bo'ladi?</problem>
<choices>A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 6</choices>
<solution>Ifodani tahlil qilamiz: $F = (A \lor \neg B) \land (B \lor C)$.
Barcha 8 ta holatni tekshiramiz:
1) (0,0,0): $(0 \lor 1) \land (0 \lor 0) = 1 \land 0 = 0$
2) (0,0,1): $(0 \lor 1) \land (0 \lor 1) = 1 \land 1 = 1$
3) (0,1,0): $(0 \lor 0) \land (1 \lor 0) = 0 \land 1 = 0$
4) (0,1,1): $(0 \lor 0) \land (1 \lor 1) = 0 \land 1 = 0$
5) (1,0,0): $(1 \lor 1) \land (0 \lor 0) = 1 \land 0 = 0$
6) (1,0,1): $(1 \lor 1) \land (0 \lor 1) = 1 \land 1 = 1$
7) (1,1,0): $(1 \lor 0) \land (1 \lor 0) = 1 \land 1 = 1$
8) (1,1,1): $(1 \lor 0) \land (1 \lor 1) = 1 \land 1 = 1$
Natijada 4 ta holatda (001, 101, 110, 111) ifoda qiymati 1 ga teng.</solution>

4. Uzatish vaqti:
$t = \dfrac{3\ 528\ 000}{35\ 280} = 100 \text{ sekund}$.</solution>

<problem>Rekursiv funksiya quyidagicha aniqlangan:
$F(n) = 1$, agar $n \le 1$ bo'lsa;
$F(n) = F(n-1) + 2 \cdot F(n-2)$, agar $n > 1$ bo'lsa.
Ushbu funksiyaning $F(6)$ qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 31  B) 43  C) 65  D) 21</choices>
<solution>Funksiyaning ketma-ket qiymatlarini hisoblaymiz:

F(1) = 1
F(2) = F(1) + 2·F(0) = 1 + 2(1) = 3
F(3) = F(2) + 2·F(1) = 3 + 2(1) = 5
F(4) = F(3) + 2·F(2) = 5 + 2(3) = 11
F(5) = F(4) + 2·F(3) = 11 + 2(5) = 21
F(6) = F(5) + 2·F(4) = 21 + 2(11) = 43.</solution>

  01000000

Demak, tarmoq manzili `192.168.10.64` bo'ladi.</solution>

<choices>A) 0  B) 2  C) 6  D) 8</choices>
<solution>7 ning darajalari oxirgi raqamining davriyligini aniqlaymiz:
$7^1 \equiv 7 \pmod{10}$
$7^2 \equiv 9 \pmod{10}$
$7^3 \equiv 3 \pmod{10}$
$7^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{10}$

$7^{2026} \equiv 7^2 \equiv 9 \pmod{10}$.
U holda $N = 7^{2026} + 3 \equiv 9 + 3 = 12 \equiv 2 \pmod{10}$.
Qoldiq 2 ga teng.</solution>

<diagram_spec>Triangle ABC. Point D lies on AB, point E lies on AC such that DE is parallel to BC. Area of ADE = 18 cm^2, area of quadrilateral DBCE = 32 cm^2. Length of segment AD = 6 cm.</diagram_spec>
<problem>ABC uchburchakda AB tomonda D nuqta va AC tomonda E nuqta shunday olindiki, DE kesma BC tomonga parallel. Agar ADE uchburchakning yuzi $18\text{ cm}^2$, DBCE to'rtburchakning yuzi $32\text{ cm}^2$ hamda $AD = 6\text{ cm}$ bo'lsa, $BD$ kesma uzunligini toping (cm da).</problem>
<solution>1. ABC uchburchakning umumiy yuzi: $S_{ABC} = S_{ADE} + S_{DBCE} = 18 + 32 = 50\text{ cm}^2$.
2. $DE \parallel BC$ bo'lgani uchun $\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$.
3. Yuzlar nisbati o'xshashlik katsientining kvadratiga teng:
$\left(\dfrac{AD}{AB}\right)^2 = \dfrac{S_{ADE}}{S_{ABC}} = \dfrac{18}{50} = \dfrac{9}{25}$.
4. Bundan $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ kelib chiqadi.
5. $AD = 6$ cm ekanidan $AB = 6 \cdot \dfrac{5}{3} = 10\text{ cm}$.
6. $BD = AB - AD = 10 - 6 = 4\text{ cm}$.</solution>
<answer>4</answer>
