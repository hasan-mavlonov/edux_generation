<problem>Agar $a+b=10$ va $a\cdot b=21$ bo'lsa, $a^2+b^2$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 58  B) 79  C) 100  D) 42</choices>
<solution>Qisqa ko'paytirish formulalaridan foydalanamiz: $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$.
Bizga kerakli qismni ajratib olamiz: $a^2+b^2 = (a+b)^2 - 2ab$. 
Berilgan qiymatlarni o'rniga qo'yamiz: $a^2+b^2 = 10^2 - 2\cdot 21 = 100 - 42 = 58$.</solution>
<answer>A</answer>

<problem>ABC teng yonli uchburchakda ($AB=BC$). $BC$ tomonda $D$ nuqta olingan bo'lib, $AD = AB$ tenglik bajariladi. Agar $\angle DAC = 15^\circ$ bo'lsa, $\angle B$ burchakning kattaligini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 40°  B) 55°  C) 70°  D) 85°</choices>
<solution>Uchburchak $ABC$ teng yonli ($AB=BC$) bo'lgani uchun uning asosidagi burchaklari teng: $\angle BAC = \angle BCA = \alpha$.
$\angle DAC = 15^\circ$ ekanligidan $\angle DAB = \alpha - 15^\circ$ bo'ladi.
Uchburchak $ABD$ ham teng yonli ($AD = AB$) bo'lgani uchun, asosidagi burchaklari teng bo'ladi: $\angle ABD = \angle ADB$. $\angle ABD$ bu $\angle B$ burchakning o'zi.
Uchburchak $ABC$ dagi burchaklar yig'indisidan: $\angle B = 180^\circ - 2\alpha$.
Demak, $\angle ADB = 180^\circ - 2\alpha$.
Uchburchak $ABD$ ning ichki burchaklari yig'indisi: $\angle DAB + \angle ABD + \angle ADB = 180^\circ$.
Qiymatlarni o'rniga qo'yamiz: $(\alpha - 15^\circ) + (180^\circ - 2\alpha) + (180^\circ - 2\alpha) = 180^\circ$.
Soddalashtiramiz: $\alpha - 15 + 360 - 4\alpha = 180 \Rightarrow 3\alpha = 165 \Rightarrow \alpha = 55^\circ$.
Nihoyat, $\angle B = 180^\circ - 2(55^\circ) = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$.</solution>
<answer>C</answer>

<problem>$p$, $q$ va $r$ turli tub sonlar bo'lib, $p \cdot q \cdot r = 5(p + q + r)$ tenglikni qanoatlantiradi. $p^2 + q^2 + r^2$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<solution>Tenglikning o'ng tomonida 5 tub soni bor, demak chap tomondagi $p, q, r$ sonlaridan biri albatta 5 ga teng bo'lishi shart.
Faraz qilaylik, $p = 5$. Tenglikka qo'yamiz:
$5 \cdot q \cdot r = 5(5 + q + r)$
$q \cdot r = 5 + q + r$
$q \cdot r - q - r = 5$
Tenglikning ikkala tomoniga 1 qo'shamiz:
$q \cdot r - q - r + 1 = 6$
$(q-1)(r-1) = 6$
q va r - tub sonlar. 6 ning ko'paytuvchilari 1x6 yoki 2x3.
1-holat: $q-1=1$ va $r-1=6 \Rightarrow q=2$ va $r=7$. Barcha {5, 2, 7} sonlar turli tub sonlardir.
2-holat: $q-1=2$ va $r-1=3 \Rightarrow q=3$ va $r=4$. Lekin 4 tub son emas.
Demak, biz qidirgan turli tub sonlar: 2, 5 va 7 ekan.
Ularning kvadratlari yig'indisi: $p^2 + q^2 + r^2 = 2^2 + 5^2 + 7^2 = 4 + 25 + 49 = 78$.</solution>
<answer>78</answer>

<problem>128 sahifali elektron hujjatning har bir sahifasi 64 qatordan, har bir qator esa 64 ta belgidan iborat. Agar barcha belgilar Unicode (1 ta belgi = 16 bit) standartida kodlangan bo'lsa, ushbu hujjat kompyuter xotirasida qancha joy (Kilobaytda) egallaydi?</problem>
<choices>A) 512  B) 1024  C) 2048  D) 256</choices>
<solution>Hujjatdagi jami belgilar sonini hisoblaymiz:
$K = 128 \times 64 \times 64 = 2^7 \times 2^6 \times 2^6 = 2^{19}$ ta belgi.
1 ta belgi Unicode'da 16 bit joy egallaydi. 16 bit = 2 Bayt = $2^1$ Bayt.
Jami hajmi (Baytlarda): $V = 2^{19} \times 2^1 = 2^{20}$ Bayt.
Hajmni Kilobayt (KB) ga o'tkazish uchun 1024 ga ($2^{10}$ ga) bo'lamiz:
$V_{KB} = 2^{20} / 2^{10} = 2^{10}$ KB = 1024 KB.</solution>
<answer>B</answer>
