a = 0

    end for

```</problem>

- $i=5$: ichki siklda $1+2+3+4+5=15$ qo'shiladi $\implies a = 10 + 15 = 25$. Sikldan so'ng: $a = 25 - 5 = 20$.

   - $b = 3 \implies c = 8 \implies (1, 3, 8)$

   - $b = 4 \implies c = 5 \implies (3, 4, 5)$ (Jami 1 ta)
