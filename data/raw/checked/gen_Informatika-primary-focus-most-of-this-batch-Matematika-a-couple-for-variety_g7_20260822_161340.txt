<problem>$\dfrac{n^2 + 4n + 25}{n + 2}$ ifodaning qiymati butun son bo'ladigan barcha butun $n$ sonlarining yig'indisini toping.</problem>
<solution>Ifoda ko'rinishini o'zgartiramiz: $\dfrac{n^2 + 4n + 25}{n + 2} = \dfrac{(n+2)^2 + 21}{n + 2} = n + 2 + \dfrac{21}{n + 2}$. Kasr butun son bo'lishi uchun $n+2$ soni 21 ning bo'luvchisi bo'lishi kerak. 21 ning barcha butun bo'luvchilari: $\pm 1, \pm 3, \pm 7, \pm 21$. $k = n+2$ desak, $n = k - 2$ bo'ladi. Barcha $k$ lar yig'indisi 0 ga teng. Demak, barcha $n$ lar yig'indisi $\sum (k - 2) = \sum k - 8 \cdot 2 = 0 - 16 = -16$.</solution>
<answer>-16</answer>
</example>

<choices>A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 5</choices>
<solution>Ifoda 1 bo'lishi uchun barcha uchta qavs bir vaqtda 1 bo'lishi kerak.
1) Agar $C = 0$ bo'lsa: $(\neg A \lor 0) = \neg A$, demak $A = 0$; $(B \lor 0) = B$, demak $B = 1$. Lekin u holda birinchi qavs $(0 \lor 0) = 0$ bo'lib qoladi. Demak, $C=0$ bo'lganda yechim yo'q.
2) Agar $C = 1$ bo'lsa: ikkinchi va uchinchi qavslar har doim 1 bo'ladi. Birinchi qavs $(A \lor \neg B)$ 1 bo'lishi uchun $(A, B)$ juftligi $(0,0), (1,0), (1,1)$ bo'lishi kerak.
Shuning uchun $(A, B, C)$ ning 3 ta holatida: $(0,0,1), (1,0,1), (1,1,1)$ ifoda Rost bo'ladi.</solution>

<problem>8 ta elementdan iborat $A = [4, 7, 2, 9, 5, 1, 8, 3]$ massiv ustida quyidagi algoritm bajarildi:
$i$ o'zgaruvchi 1 dan 7 gacha ortib boradi: agar $A[i] > A[i+1]$ bo'lsa, $A[i]$ va $A[i+1]$ elementlarning o'rinlari almashtiriladi (swap).
Ushbu sikl to'liq bajarilgandan so'ng, massivning eng oxirgi elementi $A[8]$ va eng birinchi elementi $A[1]$ ning ayirmasi ($A[8] - A[1]$) nechaga teng bo'ladi?</problem>
<solution>Algoritm qadamlarini ketma-ket bajaramiz:
- $i=1$: $A[1]=4, A[2]=7 \implies 4<7$, almashtirilmaydi. Massiv: $[4, 7, 2, 9, 5, 1, 8, 3]$
- $i=2$: $A[2]=7, A[3]=2 \implies 7>2$, swap: $[4, 2, 7, 9, 5, 1, 8, 3]$
- $i=3$: $A[3]=7, A[4]=9 \implies 7<9$, almashtirilmaydi. Massiv: $[4, 2, 7, 9, 5, 1, 8, 3]$
- $i=4$: $A[4]=9, A[5]=5 \implies 9>5$, swap: $[4, 2, 7, 5, 9, 1, 8, 3]$
- $i=5$: $A[5]=9, A[6]=1 \implies 9>1$, swap: $[4, 2, 7, 5, 1, 9, 8, 3]$
- $i=6$: $A[6]=9, A[7]=8 \implies 9>8$, swap: $[4, 2, 7, 5, 1, 8, 9, 3]$
- $i=7$: $A[7]=9, A[8]=3 \implies 9>3$, swap: $[4, 2, 7, 5, 1, 8, 3, 9]$
Natijaviy massivda $A[1] = 4$ va $A[8] = 9$. Ayirma: $A[8] - A[1] = 9 - 4 = 5$.</solution>
<answer>5</answer>
</example>
