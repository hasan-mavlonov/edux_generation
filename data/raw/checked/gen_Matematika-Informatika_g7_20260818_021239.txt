<problem>Anvar kitob o'qimoqda. U birinchi kuni kitobning 1/3 qismini, ikkinchi kuni qolgan qismining 2/5 qismini, uchinchi kuni esa qolgan barcha 60 ta sahifani o'qib tugatdi. Kitob jami nechta sahifadan iborat?</problem>
<solution>Kitobning jami sahifalari sonini $x$ deb belgilaymiz.

Kitob jami 150 sahifadan iborat.</solution>

<problem>Qanday eng katta natural $n$ soni uchun $\frac{n^2+7n+34}{n+3}$ kasrning qiymati natural son bo'ladi?</problem>
<choices>A) 8  B) 19  C) 22  D) 11</choices>
<solution>Kasrning suratini quyidagicha ajratib olamiz:
$n^2 + 7n + 34 = n^2 + 3n + 4n + 12 + 22 = n(n+3) + 4(n+3) + 22 = (n+3)(n+4) + 22$.
Kasrni alohida bo'laklarga ajratib yozamiz:
$\frac{(n+3)(n+4) + 22}{n+3} = n + 4 + \frac{22}{n+3}$.
Kasrning qiymati natural son bo'lishi uchun $\frac{22}{n+3}$ ifoda butun son bo'lishi kerak. Ya'ni $n+3$ soni 22 ning bo'luvchisi bo'lishi lozim.
22 ning musbat bo'luvchilari: 1, 2, 11, 22.
$n \ge 1$ (natural son) bo'lgani uchun $n+3 \ge 4$ bo'ladi.
Demak, $n+3$ faqat 11 yoki 22 bo'lishi mumkin.
Bizdan eng katta $n$ ni topish so'ralgan:
$n+3 = 22 \implies n = 19$. (Bu holda ifoda qiymati $19 + 4 + 1 = 24$, bu natural son).
Eng katta natural son $n=19$.</solution>

$\angle ADC + \angle DAC + \angle C = 180^\circ$

<choices>A) 120  B) 180  C) 200  D) 250</choices>
<solution>Faraz qilaylik, poezdning uzunligi $L$ (metr), uning tezligi esa $v$ (m/s) ga teng.
Poezdning simyog'och yonidan o'tishi uning faqat o'z uzunligini bosib o'tishiga teng: 
$L = v \cdot 15$. (1)
Poezdning ko'prikdan o'tishi uning o'z uzunligi va ko'prik uzunligini birgalikda bosib o'tishiga teng: 
$L + 300 = v \cdot 40$. (2)

$15v + 300 = 40v \implies 40v - 15v = 300 \implies 25v = 300 \implies v = 12$ (m/s).
Tezlikni bilgan holda poezdning uzunligini topamiz:
$L = 15 \cdot 12 = 180$ metr.</solution>

<solution>Fayl yuklab olish vaqtini topish uchun fayl hajmini tarmoq tezligiga bo'lish kerak, bunda ularni bir xil o'lchov birligiga (bitlarga) keltiramiz.

Qisqartirishlarni bajaramiz ($1024$ lar qisqarib ketadi):
$T = \frac{15 \cdot 1024 \cdot 8}{512}$
$1024$ ni $512$ ga bo'lsak $2$ hosil bo'ladi:
$T = 15 \cdot 2 \cdot 8 = 30 \cdot 8 = 240$ soniya.</solution>

<solution>Qutidagi olmalar sonini $a$, noklar sonini $n$ deb belgilaymiz.

$100a + 150n = 5500$.

$100a + 150(45 - a) = 5500 \implies 100a + 6750 - 150a = 5500$
$6750 - 5500 = 50a \implies 1250 = 50a \implies a = 25$.
Demak, qutida 25 ta olma bor ekan.</solution>
