<problem>Qishloqdagi 64 ta uyga unikal ikkilik kodlar berilishi kerak. Har bir kodning uzunligi bir xil bo'lib, ikkita ketma-ket 1 raqami ishlatilishi taqiqlangan (masalan, 1010 joiz, lekin 1100 taqiqlangan). Ushbu shartni qanoatlantirgan holda barcha 64 ta uyni kodlash uchun eng kamida necha bitlik kod uzunligi talab qilinadi?</problem>
<choices>A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 9</choices>

$n=7 \implies F_9 = 34$ ta kod;
$n=8 \implies F_{10} = 55$ ta kod (64 ta uy uchun yetarli emas);
$n=9 \implies F_{11} = 89$ ta kod ($89 \ge 64$, yetarli).
Demak, eng kamida 9 bitlik kod kerak.</solution>

<problem>Agar $x + \dfrac{1}{x} = 5$ bo'lsa, $x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2}$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 23  B) 25  C) 27  D) 21</choices>
<solution>$x + \dfrac{1}{x} = 5$ tenglikning ikkala tarafini kvadratga oshiramiz:
$\left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2 = 5^2 \implies x^2 + 2 \cdot x \cdot \dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x^2} = 25 \implies x^2 + 2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2} = 25$.
Natijada $x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2} = 25 - 2 = 23$.</solution>

<problem>7 ga bo'lganda 3 qoldiq, 5 ga bo'lganda esa 2 qoldiq beradigan eng kichik ikki xonali natural sonni toping.</problem>
<solution>Qidirilayotgan son $n$ bo'lsin. Shartga ko'ra $n = 7k + 3$ ko'rinishida bo'ladi.
$k=0 \implies n=3$ (bir xonali son);
$k=1 \implies n=10$, $10 \pmod 5 = 0$;
$k=2 \implies n=17$, $17 \pmod 5 = 2$.
17 soni 7 ga bo'linganda 3 qoldiq ($17 = 7 \cdot 2 + 3$) va 5 ga bo'linganda 2 qoldiq ($17 = 5 \cdot 3 + 2$) beradi. Demak, eng kichik ikki xonali son 17 dir.</solution>
<answer>17</answer>
</example>

<problem>Teng yonli $ABC$ uchburchakda ($AB = AC$), $AC$ yon tomonda $D$ nuqta shunday olinganki, $AD = BD = BC$ tenglik bajariladi. $\angle ABC$ burchakning kattaligini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 36°  B) 54°  C) 72°  D) 108°</choices>
<solution>$\angle A = x$ bo'lsin. $\triangle ABD$ teng yonli ($AD = BD$), shuning uchun $\angle ABD = \angle A = x$.
Tashqi burchak xossasiga ko'ra $\angle BDC = \angle A + \angle ABD = 2x$.
$\triangle BCD$ teng yonli ($BD = BC$), demak $\angle C = \angle BDC = 2x$.
Boshida $AB = AC$ berilgan edi, shuning uchun $\angle ABC = \angle C = 2x$.
$ABC$ uchburchak ichki burchaklari yig'indisi: $x + 2x + 2x = 180^\circ \implies 5x = 180^\circ \implies x = 36^\circ$.
Shunda $\angle ABC = 2x = 72^\circ$.</solution>
