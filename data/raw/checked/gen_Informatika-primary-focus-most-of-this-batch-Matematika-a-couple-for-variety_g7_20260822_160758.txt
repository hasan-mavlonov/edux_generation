<problem>$n$ ning nechta natural qiymatida $\dfrac{3n + 23}{n + 2}$ ifodaning qiymati natural son bo'ladi?</problem>
<solution>Kasrning butun qismini ajratamiz:
$\dfrac{3n + 23}{n + 2} = \dfrac{3(n + 2) + 17}{n + 2} = 3 + \dfrac{17}{n + 2}$.
Natija natural son bo'lishi uchun $n + 2$ soni 17 ning bo'luvchisi bo'lishi kerak. 17 — tub son, uning natural bo'luvchilari 1 va 17.
$n$ natural son ($n \ge 1$) bo'lgani uchun $n + 2 \ge 3$.
Shuning uchun $n + 2 = 17 \implies n = 15$.
$n + 2 = 1$ tenglama $n = -1$ beradi, bu natural son emas.
Demak, $n$ ning faqat 1 ta natural qiymati ($n = 15$) mos keladi.</solution>
<answer>1</answer>

<problem>$F(A, B, C) = (A \text{ AND } B) \text{ OR } (A \text{ AND } \text{NOT}(B)) \text{ OR } (A \text{ AND } C)$ mantiqiy ifodasi quyidagilardan qaysi biriga teng kuchli (ekvivalent)?</problem>
<choices>A) A  B) A AND B  C) A OR C  D) B AND C</choices>
<solution>Mantiqiy algebra qonunlaridan foydalanib soddalashtiramiz:
$F = A \text{ AND } (B \text{ OR } \text{NOT}(B)) \text{ OR } (A \text{ AND } C)$.
$B \text{ OR } \text{NOT}(B) = 1$ bo'lgani uchun:
$F = (A \text{ AND } 1) \text{ OR } (A \text{ AND } C) = A \text{ OR } (A \text{ AND } C)$.
Yutilish qonuniga ko'ra $A \text{ OR } (A \text{ AND } C) = A$.
Natija: A.</solution>
<answer>A</answer>
