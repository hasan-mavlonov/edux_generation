<problem>$\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{y} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ tenglamani qanoatlantiradigan $x \le y$ shartni bajaruvchi barcha natural $(x, y)$ juftliklar sonini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 9</choices>
<solution>Tenglamani almashtiramiz: $6(x+y) = xy \implies xy - 6x - 6y + 36 = 36 \implies (x-6)(y-6) = 36$.
$x$ va $y$ natural sonlar bo'lgani uchun $x-6$ va $y-6$ ko'paytmasi 36 bo'lgan butun sonlar bo'ladi. $x \le y$ shartdan $x-6 \le y-6$ kelib chiqadi.
36 ning musbat ko'paytuvchilar juftliklari ($u \le v$):
1) $(1, 36) \implies x=7, y=42$
2) $(2, 18) \implies x=8, y=24$
3) $(3, 12) \implies x=9, y=18$
4) $(4, 9) \implies x=10, y=15$
5) $(6, 6) \implies x=12, y=12$
Manfiy ko'paytuvchilar olinganda $y \le 0$ bo'lib, natural yechim hosil bo'lmaydi. Demak, jami 5 ta juftlik bor.</solution>
<answer>C</answer>
</example>

Demak, tarmoq manzili: `192.168.1.128`.</solution>
