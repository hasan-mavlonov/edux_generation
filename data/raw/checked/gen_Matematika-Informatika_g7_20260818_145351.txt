<problem>$x=2025$ bo'lsa, $\frac{x^3 + 1}{x^2 - 2024} - 2024$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 1  B) 2  C) 2025  D) 2026</choices>
<solution>We substitute $x=2025$ into the denominator. Notice that $2024 = 2025 - 1 = x - 1$. So the denominator becomes $x^2 - (x - 1) = x^2 - x + 1$. 
The fraction is $\frac{x^3 + 1}{x^2 - x + 1}$. Using the sum of cubes identity, $x^3 + 1 = (x + 1)(x^2 - x + 1)$.
Thus, the fraction simplifies to $x + 1$. 
The entire expression becomes $(x + 1) - 2024$. 
Since $x = 2025$, this is $(2025 + 1) - 2024 = 2026 - 2024 = 2$.</solution>
<answer>B</answer>

<problem>Ikkilik sanoq sistemasidagi $101101_2$ va $11011_2$ sonlarining yig'indisini toping va natijadagi birlar (1) sonini aniqlang.</problem>
<choices>A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 5</choices>
<solution>First, convert the binary numbers to decimal to perform the addition easily (or add them directly in binary). 
$101101_2 = 32 + 0 + 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 45$.
$11011_2 = 16 + 8 + 0 + 2 + 1 = 27$.
Sum in decimal: $45 + 27 = 72$.
Now, convert 72 back to binary: $72 = 64 + 8 = 2^6 + 2^3$.
In binary, this is $1001000_2$.
Counting the number of 1s in the binary representation, we have exactly two 1s.</solution>

<problem>Raqamlari yig'indisi 9 ga teng bo'lgan va 5 ga qoldiqsiz bo'linadigan nechta to'rt xonali natural son mavjud?</problem>
<solution>A 4-digit number $\overline{abcd}$ is divisible by 5 if $d=0$ or $d=5$. Also, $a \ge 1$.
Case 1: $d=0$. The sum of remaining digits is $a+b+c = 9$. Let $a' = a - 1 \ge 0$. Then $a'+b+c = 8$. The number of non-negative integer solutions is $\binom{8 + 3 - 1}{3 - 1} = \binom{10}{2} = 45$.
Case 2: $d=5$. The sum of remaining digits is $a+b+c = 4$. Let $a' = a - 1 \ge 0$. Then $a'+b+c = 3$. The number of non-negative integer solutions is $\binom{3 + 3 - 1}{3 - 1} = \binom{5}{2} = 10$.
Total number of such numbers = $45 + 10 = 55$.</solution>
<answer>55</answer>

<solution>We analyze the cycle of last digits for powers of 7 and 3.
Powers of 7 end in: 7, 9, 3, 1... (cycle length 4).
Powers of 3 end in: 3, 9, 7, 1... (cycle length 4).
Since $2025 = 4 \times 506 + 1$, the exponent 2025 leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 4.
Therefore, the last digit of $7^{2025}$ is the 1st number in its cycle, which is 7.
Similarly, the last digit of $3^{2025}$ is the 1st number in its cycle, which is 3.
Adding the last digits gives $7 + 3 = 10$. The final digit of the sum is 0.</solution>

<problem>Uzunligi 400 metr bo'lgan aylanasimon yugurish yo'lakchasida ikki yuguruvchi bir nuqtadan bir vaqtda yugurishni boshladi. Agar ular qarama-qarshi yo'nalishda yugursa, har 40 soniyada uchrashadilar. Agar ular bir xil yo'nalishda yugursa, tez yuguruvchi sekin yuguruvchini har 200 soniyada quvib yetadi. Tez yuguruvchining tezligini (m/s) toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 8</choices>
<solution>Let the speeds of the fast and slow runners be $v_1$ and $v_2$ m/s, respectively ($v_1 > v_2$).
When running in opposite directions, their relative speed is $v_1 + v_2$. They cover a combined distance of 400 m in 40 s:
$v_1 + v_2 = 400 / 40 = 10$ m/s.
When running in the same direction, their relative speed is $v_1 - v_2$. The faster runner covers an extra lap of 400 m in 200 s:
$v_1 - v_2 = 400 / 200 = 2$ m/s.
Adding the two equations: $2v_1 = 12 \implies v_1 = 6$ m/s.
Subtracting them gives $2v_2 = 8 \implies v_2 = 4$ m/s. The faster runner's speed is 6 m/s.</solution>
<answer>C</answer>

Part 1 (first 30 MB) time: 30 MB / 3 MB/s = 10 seconds.
Remaining file size: 150 MB - 30 MB = 120 MB.
Part 2 (remaining 120 MB) time: 120 MB / 2 MB/s = 60 seconds.
Total time = 10 s + 60 s = 70 seconds.</solution>
