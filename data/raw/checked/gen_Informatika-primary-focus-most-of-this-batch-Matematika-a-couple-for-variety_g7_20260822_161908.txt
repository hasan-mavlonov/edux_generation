<problem>$n^2 + 5n + 25$ ifoda biror butun sonning kvadrati (to'liq kvadrat) bo'ladigan eng kichik natural $n$ sonini toping.</problem>
<solution>Ifodani to'liq kvadratlar bilan solishtiramiz.
$(n+2)^2 = n^2 + 4n + 4$. Natural $n \ge 1$ uchun $n^2 + 5n + 25 > n^2 + 4n + 4 = (n+2)^2$.
Endi $n^2 + 5n + 25 = (n+k)^2$ tenglikni $k \ge 3$ holatlar uchun ko'rib chiqamiz:
1) Agar $k = 4$ bo'lsa: $(n+4)^2 = n^2 + 8n + 16$.
$n^2 + 5n + 25 = n^2 + 8n + 16 \implies 3n = 9 \implies n = 3$.
Tekshiramiz: $3^2 + 5\cdot3 + 25 = 9 + 15 + 25 = 49 = 7^2$ (to'liq kvadrat).
2) Smallest natural $n$ ni izlayotganimiz uchun $n=1$ va $n=2$ ni tekshiramiz:
$n=1 \implies 1+5+25 = 31$ (kvadrat emas).
$n=2 \implies 4+10+25 = 39$ (kvadrat emas).
Demak, eng kichik natural son $n = 3$.</solution>
<answer>3</answer>
</problem_item>
