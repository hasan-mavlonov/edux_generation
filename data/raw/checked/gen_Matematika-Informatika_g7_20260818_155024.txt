<problem>$n$ natural sonining teng yarmi biror butun sonning kvadrati, uchdan bir qismi biror butun sonning kubi va beshdan bir qismi biror butun sonning beshinchi darajasiga teng. Eng kichik shunday $n$ soni $n = 2^a \cdot 3^b \cdot 5^c$ ko'rinishida yozilishi mumkin bo'lsa, $a+b+c$ yig'indini toping.</problem>
<solution>$n = 2^a \cdot 3^b \cdot 5^c$ shaklida qidiramiz.
1) $n/2 = 2^{a-1} 3^b 5^c$ kvadrat bo'lishi uchun darajalar juft bo'lishi kerak: $a-1 \equiv 0 \pmod 2 \implies a$ toq; $b$ juft; $c$ juft.
2) $n/3 = 2^a 3^{b-1} 5^c$ kub bo'lishi uchun darajalar 3 ga karrali bo'lishi kerak: $a \equiv 0 \pmod 3$; $b-1 \equiv 0 \pmod 3 \implies b \equiv 1 \pmod 3$; $c \equiv 0 \pmod 3$.
3) $n/5 = 2^a 3^b 5^{c-1}$ beshinchi daraja bo'lishi uchun: $a \equiv 0 \pmod 5$; $b \equiv 0 \pmod 5$; $c-1 \equiv 0 \pmod 5 \implies c \equiv 1 \pmod 5$.
Endi har bir daraja uchun eng kichik qiymatlarni topamiz:
$a$: toq, 3 va 5 ga bo'linadi. 3 va 5 ning EKUKi 15. Eng kichik toq son: $a = 15$.
$b$: juft, 5 ga bo'linadi (demak 10 ga karrali) va 3 ga bo'lganda qoldiq 1. 10 ga karrali bo'lgan eng kichik shunday son: $b = 10$.
$c$: juft, 3 ga bo'linadi (demak 6 ga karrali) va 5 ga bo'lganda qoldiq 1. 6 ga karrali bo'lgan eng kichik shunday son: $c = 6$.
Natija: $a+b+c = 15 + 10 + 6 = 31$.</solution>
