<problem>Agar $x - \frac{1}{x} = 5$ bo'lsa, $x^3 - \frac{1}{x^3}$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 115  B) 125  C) 140  D) 110</choices>
<solution>Qisqa ko'paytirish formulalaridan $a^3 - b^3 = (a-b)^3 + 3ab(a-b)$ ni qo'llaymiz. Bu yerda $a=x$ va $b=\frac{1}{x}$.
Formula bo'yicha: $x^3 - \frac{1}{x^3} = \left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^3 + 3 \cdot x \cdot \frac{1}{x} \cdot \left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)$.
Tenglikdagi $x \cdot \frac{1}{x} = 1$ qisqaradi. Berilgan $x - \frac{1}{x} = 5$ qiymatni o'rniga qo'yamiz:
$5^3 + 3 \cdot 1 \cdot 5 = 125 + 15 = 140$.</solution>
<answer>C</answer>
</example>

<problem>ABC uchburchakning BC tomonida D nuqta olingan bo'lib, ular orasidagi nisbat $BD:DC = 2:5$ ni tashkil etadi. AD kesmaning o'rtasida E nuqta yotadi. Agar ABC uchburchakning yuzi $140 \text{ sm}^2$ bo'lsa, CDE uchburchakning yuzini toping ($sm^2$).</problem>
<solution>1) ABC uchburchakning BC tomoni jami $2 + 5 = 7$ qismga bo'lingan. ADC uchburchak yuzi butun uchburchak yuzining BC tomondagi DC kesmaga mutanosib qismiga teng, chunki balandliklari bir xil. 
$S_{ADC} = \frac{DC}{BC} S_{ABC} = \frac{5}{7} \cdot 140 = 100 \text{ sm}^2$.
2) ADC uchburchakda CE kesma o'tkazilgan va E nuqta AD tomonning o'rtasi bo'lganligi sababli, CE kesma ADC uchburchakning medianasi hisoblanadi. Mediana uchburchak yuzini teng ikkiga bo'ladi.
$S_{CDE} = \frac{1}{2} S_{ADC} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 100 = 50 \text{ sm}^2$.</solution>
<answer>50</answer>
