Boshlang'ich qiymatlar: $a_1 = 2$ ('0', '1'), $a_2 = 3$ ('00', '01', '10').
Ketma-ket hisoblaymiz: $a_3 = 5$, $a_4 = 8$, $a_5 = 13$, $a_6 = 21$.</solution>

4) Qiymatlarni qo'yamiz: $7 \cdot 18 = x^5 + \dfrac{1}{x^5} + 3 \implies 126 = x^5 + \dfrac{1}{x^5} + 3 \implies x^5 + \dfrac{1}{x^5} = 123$.</solution>
<answer>123</answer>
</example>
