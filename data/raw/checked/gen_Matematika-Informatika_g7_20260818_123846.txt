<problem>Quyidagi mantiqiy ifodani soddalashtiring (bunda $\cdot$ — AND (ko'paytirish), $+$ — OR (qo'shish), ustki chiziq $\overline{X}$ — NOT (inkor) amalini bildiradi):
F = $\overline{(\overline{A} + B)} + (A \cdot B)$</problem>
<choices>A) $A$  B) $B$  C) $A + B$  D) $1$</choices>
<solution>Ifodaning birinchi qismiga De Morgan qonunini qo'llaymiz: $\overline{(X + Y)} = \overline{X} \cdot \overline{Y}$.
Shunga ko'ra: $\overline{(\overline{A} + B)} = \overline{\overline{A}} \cdot \overline{B}$.
Ikki marta inkor amali qisqaradi: $\overline{\overline{A}} = A$. Demak, birinchi qism $A \cdot \overline{B}$ ga teng bo'ladi.
To'liq ifodani yozamiz: F = $A \cdot \overline{B} + A \cdot B$.
Bu ifodadan $A$ ni qavsdan tashqariga chiqaramiz: F = $A \cdot (\overline{B} + B)$.
Mantiqiy qonunlarga ko'ra, har qanday o'zgaruvchining o'zining inkori bilan OR (+) amali 1 ga (Rost) teng: $\overline{B} + B = 1$.
Natija: F = $A \cdot 1 = A$.</solution>
