<problem>$n^2 + 14n - 15$ ifodaning qiymati biror natural sonning kvadrati bo'ladigan barcha natural $n$ sonlarining yig'indisini toping.</problem>
<solution>Tenglamani tuzamiz: $n^2 + 14n - 15 = k^2$, bunda $k \in \mathbb{N}$. To'la kvadratga ajratamiz: $(n+7)^2 - 64 = k^2 \implies (n+7)^2 - k^2 = 64 \implies (n+7-k)(n+7+k) = 64$. $n, k$ natural sonlar bo'lgani uchun ikkala ko'paytuvchi ham bir xil juftlikka ega bo'lgan musbat juft sonlar bo'lishi kerak ($n+7+k > n+7-k > 0$). 64 ning bunday ko'paytuvchilar juftliklari: 1) $n+7+k = 32$ va $n+7-k = 2 \implies 2(n+7) = 34 \implies n = 10, k = 15$. 2) $n+7+k = 16$ va $n+7-k = 4 \implies 2(n+7) = 20 \implies n = 3, k = 6$. 3) $n+7+k = 8$ va $n+7-k = 8 \implies n = 1, k = 0$ ($k$ natural son emas). Shunday qilib, natural $n$ ning qiymatlari $n = 10$ va $n = 3$. Ularning yig'indisi: $10 + 3 = 13$.</solution>
<answer>13</answer>
</example>

<problem>$P(x) = x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c$ ko'phad uchun $P(1) = 4$, $P(2) = 7$ va $P(3) = 12$ ekani ma'lum. $P(5) - P(-1)$ ayirmaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 72  B) 68  C) 80  D) 64</choices>
<solution>E'tibor beraylik: $1^2 + 3 = 4$, $2^2 + 3 = 7$, $3^2 + 3 = 12$. Demak, $P(x) - (x^2 + 3)$ ko'phadning ildizlari 1, 2 va 3 dir. $P(x)$ uchinchi darajali va bosh koeffitsiyenti 1 bo'lgani uchun: $P(x) - (x^2 + 3) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)$, ya'ni $P(x) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3) + x^2 + 3$.
Endi qiymatlarni hisoblaymiz:
$P(5) = (4)(3)(2) + 5^2 + 3 = 24 + 25 + 3 = 52$.
$P(-1) = (-2)(-3)(-4) + (-1)^2 + 3 = -24 + 1 + 3 = -20$.
$P(5) - P(-1) = 52 - (-20) = 72$.</solution>
<answer>A</answer>

<solution>Tenglikning ikkala tarafini kvadratga oshiramiz:

Yana bir marta kvadratga oshiramiz:
$\left(x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)^2 = 14^2 \implies x^4 + 2 + \dfrac{1}{x^4} = 196 \implies x^4 + \dfrac{1}{x^4} = 194$.</solution>
