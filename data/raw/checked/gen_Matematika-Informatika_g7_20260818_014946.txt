$EC = y - BE = y - \frac{24}{x}$
$FC = x - DF = x - \frac{12}{y}$

$S_{\triangle ECF} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot EC \cdot FC = \frac{1}{2} (y - \frac{24}{x})(x - \frac{12}{y}) = 9$

<problem>Sonlar ketma-ketligi $a_1 = 2026$ va ixtiyoriy $n \ge 1$ uchun $a_{n+1} = \frac{a_n - 1}{a_n + 1}$ qoidasi bilan berilgan. $a_{2026} \cdot a_{2028}$ ko'paytmaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<solution>Ketma-ketlikning dastlabki hadlarini ixtiyoriy $x$ uchun hisoblab ko'ramiz (bunda $x = a_1$):
$a_1 = x$
$a_2 = \frac{x - 1}{x + 1}$
$a_3 = \frac{\frac{x - 1}{x + 1} - 1}{\frac{x - 1}{x + 1} + 1} = \frac{\frac{x - 1 - x - 1}{x + 1}}{\frac{x - 1 + x + 1}{x + 1}} = \frac{-2}{2x} = -\frac{1}{x}$
$a_4 = \frac{-\frac{1}{x} - 1}{-\frac{1}{x} + 1} = \frac{\frac{-1 - x}{x}}{\frac{-1 + x}{x}} = \frac{-(x + 1)}{-(1 - x)} = -\frac{x + 1}{x - 1}$
$a_5 = \frac{-\frac{x + 1}{x - 1} - 1}{-\frac{x + 1}{x - 1} + 1} = \frac{\frac{-x - 1 - x + 1}{x - 1}}{\frac{-x - 1 + x - 1}{x - 1}} = \frac{-2x}{-2} = x = a_1$.
Ko'rib turganimizdek, ketma-ketlik 4 davrli, ya'ni $a_{n+4} = a_n$.
Bizdan $a_{2026} \cdot a_{2028}$ ni topish so'ralmoqda.
$2026 = 4 \times 506 + 2 \implies 2026 \equiv 2 \pmod 4$. Demak, $a_{2026} = a_2$.
$2028 = 4 \times 507 + 0 \implies 2028 \equiv 4 \pmod 4$. Demak, $a_{2028} = a_4$.
Biz $a_2 \cdot a_4$ ni topishimiz kerak:
$a_2 \cdot a_4 = \left( \frac{x - 1}{x + 1} \right) \cdot \left( -\frac{x + 1}{x - 1} \right) = -1$.
Bunda $x = 2026$ ekanligining ahamiyati yo'q, ko'paytma doim -1 ga teng.</solution>
