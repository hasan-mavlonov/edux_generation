<problem>Agar $x^2 - y^2 = 40$ va $x - y = 4$ bo'lsa, $x^3 - y^3$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 316  B) 280  C) 256  D) 343</choices>
<solution>Qisqa ko'paytirish formulasidan foydalanamiz: $x^2 - y^2 = (x-y)(x+y) = 40$. Masala shartiga ko'ra $x-y = 4$ bo'lgani uchun, $4(x+y) = 40$, bundan $x+y = 10$.
Chiziqli tenglamalar sistemasini yechamiz: $x+y=10$ va $x-y=4$. Ularni qo'shsak, $2x = 14 \implies x = 7$. Ayirsak, $2y = 6 \implies y = 3$.
Bizdan $x^3 - y^3$ so'ralgan. Qiymatlarni o'rniga qo'yamiz: $7^3 - 3^3 = 343 - 27 = 316$.</solution>
<answer>A</answer>

<problem>$2^{2024} + 3^{2024}$ sonini 7 ga bo'lgandagi qoldiqni toping.</problem>
<solution>Har bir qo'shiluvchining 7 ga bo'lgandagi qoldig'ini alohida hisoblaymiz.
2 ning darajalari uchun: $2^1 \equiv 2$, $2^2 \equiv 4$, $2^3 \equiv 8 \equiv 1 \pmod 7$. Demak, $2^3$ ning 7 ga bo'lgandagi qoldig'i 1. $2024$ sonini 3 ga bo'lamiz: $2024 = 3 \cdot 674 + 2$. Shundan kelib chiqib, $2^{2024} = (2^3)^{674} \cdot 2^2 \equiv 1^{674} \cdot 4 \equiv 4 \pmod 7$.
3 ning darajalari uchun: Fermaning kichik teoremasiga ko'ra $3^6 \equiv 1 \pmod 7$. $2024$ sonini 6 ga bo'lamiz: $2024 = 6 \cdot 337 + 2$. Demak, $3^{2024} = (3^6)^{337} \cdot 3^2 \equiv 1^{337} \cdot 9 \equiv 2 \pmod 7$ (chunki $9 \pmod 7 = 2$).
Ikkala qoldiqni qo'shamiz: $4 + 2 = 6$. Yig'indi 7 dan kichik bo'lgani uchun yakuniy qoldiq 6 ga teng.</solution>
<answer>6</answer>

<problem>Chizmada $AB = AC$ bo'lgan $ABC$ teng yonli uchburchak berilgan. $BC$ asosda $D$ nuqta shunday olinganki, $AD = CD$ va $\angle BAD = 30^\circ$ tengliklar bajariladi. $\angle BAC$ burchakning kattaligini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 70°  B) 80°  C) 50°  D) 100°</choices>
<solution>$ABC$ teng yonli uchburchak bo'lgani uchun uning asosdagi burchaklari teng: $\angle B = \angle C = x$.
$ADC$ uchburchakda $AD = CD$ shart berilgan, demak bu uchburchak ham teng yonli va uning asosidagi burchaklar $\angle DAC = \angle C = x$.
Katta $ABC$ uchburchakning uchidagi burchagi $\angle BAC = \angle BAD + \angle DAC = 30^\circ + x$.
Uchburchakning ichki burchaklar yig'indisi $180^\circ$ ekanligidan foydalanamiz:
$\angle BAC + \angle B + \angle C = (30^\circ + x) + x + x = 180^\circ$.
Tenglamani yechamiz: $30^\circ + 3x = 180^\circ \implies 3x = 150^\circ \implies x = 50^\circ$.
Bizdan $\angle BAC$ ni topish so'ralgan: $\angle BAC = 30^\circ + x = 30^\circ + 50^\circ = 80^\circ$.</solution>

<problem>$a, b, c$ natural sonlar bo'lib, $ab+c=19$ va $bc+a=26$ tengliklar o'rinli ekanligi ma'lum. $a+b+c$ yig'indining qabul qilishi mumkin bo'lgan eng katta qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 13  B) 17  C) 19  D) 21</choices>
<solution>Ikkinchi tenglamadan birinchisini ayiramiz:
$(bc+a) - (ab+c) = 26 - 19 \implies bc - ab + a - c = 7$
Guruhlash orqali ko'paytuvchilarga ajratamiz: $b(c-a) - (c-a) = 7 \implies (b-1)(c-a) = 7$.
$a, b, c$ natural sonlar bo'lgani uchun ularning ayirmalari butun son. 7 tub son bo'lib, natural $b$ uchun $b-1 \ge 0$ ekanligini hisobga olsak, 7 ning ko'paytuvchilari faqat $1 \times 7$ yoki $7 \times 1$ bo'lishi mumkin.
1-holat: $b-1 = 1$ va $c-a = 7$. Bundan $b = 2$ va $c = a+7$. Bu qiymatlarni birinchi $ab+c=19$ tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $a(2) + (a+7) = 19 \implies 3a = 12 \implies a = 4$. Unda $c = 4+7 = 11$. Yig'indi: $a+b+c = 4+2+11 = 17$.
2-holat: $b-1 = 7$ va $c-a = 1$. Bundan $b = 8$ va $c = a+1$. Birinchi tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $a(8) + (a+1) = 19 \implies 9a = 18 \implies a = 2$. Unda $c = 2+1 = 3$. Yig'indi: $a+b+c = 2+8+3 = 13$.
Eng katta so'ralgan yig'indi 17 ga teng.</solution>
<answer>B</answer>

<problem>Agar $f(x)$ funksiya har bir haqiqiy $x$ soni uchun $f(x) + 2f(2025 - x) = x$ tenglikni qanoatlantirsa, $f(675)$ ning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<solution>Berilgan $f(x) + 2f(2025 - x) = x$ tenglikda dastlab $x = 675$ qiymatni qo'yamiz:
$f(675) + 2f(2025 - 675) = 675 \implies f(675) + 2f(1350) = 675$ (1-tenglama).
Endi xuddi shu tenglikka $x = 1350$ qiymatini qo'yamiz:
$f(1350) + 2f(2025 - 1350) = 1350 \implies f(1350) + 2f(675) = 1350$ (2-tenglama).
Sistemani $f(675)$ ga nisbatan yechamiz. Buning uchun 2-tenglamani 2 ga ko'paytiramiz:
$2f(1350) + 4f(675) = 2700$.
Shu ifodadan 1-tenglamani ayirib tashlaymiz:
$(4f(675) + 2f(1350)) - (f(675) + 2f(1350)) = 2700 - 675$.
$3f(675) = 2025$.
Tenglamani 3 ga bo'lsak, natija kelib chiqadi: $f(675) = 675$.</solution>

<solution>Masalada fayl hajmi Megabaytda (MB), ma'lumot uzatish tezligi esa Megabit/sekundda (Mbps) berilgan.

$15 \text{ MB} = 15 \times 8 \text{ Mb} = 120 \text{ Mb}$ (Megabit).
Faylni o'tkazish uchun sarflanadigan vaqtni $t = S / v$ formuladan topamiz:
$t = 120 / 3 = 40$ sekund.</solution>
