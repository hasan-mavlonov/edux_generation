<problem>$n$ ning nechta butun qiymatida $\dfrac{3n + 17}{n + 2}$ kasrning qiymati butun son bo'ladi?</problem>
<solution>Kasrning butun qismini ajratib olamiz:
$\dfrac{3n + 17}{n + 2} = \dfrac{3(n + 2) + 11}{n + 2} = 3 + \dfrac{11}{n + 2}$.
Bu ifoda butun son bo'lishi uchun $n + 2$ soni 11 ning bo'luvchisi bo'lishi kerak.
11 tub son bo'lgani uchun uning butun bo me'yoriy bo'luvchilari: 1, -1, 11, -11 (jami 4 ta).
Har bir bo'luvchiga mos $n$ qiymatlarini topamiz:
1) $n + 2 = 1 \implies n = -1$
2) $n + 2 = -1 \implies n = -3$
3) $n + 2 = 11 \implies n = 9$
4) $n + 2 = -11 \implies n = -13$
Demak, $n$ ning 4 ta butun qiymatida kasr qiymati butun son bo'ladi.</solution>
<answer>4</answer>
</example>
