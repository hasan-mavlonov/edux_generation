<diagram_spec>Triangle ABC with AB = AC. Point D lies on the side AC. The line segments AD, BD, and BC are all equal in length (AD = BD = BC).</diagram_spec>
<problem>ABC teng yonli uchburchakda ($AB = AC$) AC tomonida shunday D nuqta olinganki, unda $AD = BD = BC$ tenglik bajariladi. Uchburchakning A burchagini (gradusda) toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 36°  B) 45°  C) 30°  D) 72°</choices>
<solution>$\angle A = x$ deb belgilaymiz.
$\triangle ABD$ teng yonli ($AD = BD$) bo'lgani uchun uning asosidagi burchaklari teng: $\angle ABD = \angle A = x$.
Uchburchakning tashqi burchagi xossasiga ko'ra: $\angle BDC = \angle A + \angle ABD = x + x = 2x$.
$\triangle BDC$ ham teng yonli ($BD = BC$) bo'lgani uchun asosidagi burchaklari teng: $\angle C = \angle BDC = 2x$.
$\triangle ABC$ berilishiga ko'ra teng yonli ($AB = AC$), shuning uchun $\angle ABC = \angle C = 2x$.
Uchburchak ichki burchaklari yig'indisi 180° ekanligidan: $\angle A + \angle ABC + \angle C = 180^\circ \implies x + 2x + 2x = 180^\circ \implies 5x = 180^\circ \implies x = 36^\circ$.
Demak, $\angle A = 36^\circ$.</solution>

<problem>Tenglamani yeching ($x > 0$):
$\frac{1}{x(x+1)} + \frac{1}{(x+1)(x+2)} + \dots + \frac{1}{(x+9)(x+10)} = \frac{10}{39}$</problem>
<solution>Kasrlarni ajratib yozish usulidan (teleskopik yig'indi) foydalanamiz:
$\frac{1}{x(x+1)} = \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x+1}$
Shu qoidani barcha hadlarga qo'llasak, yig'indi quyidagicha soddalashadi:
$(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x+1}) + (\frac{1}{x+1} - \frac{1}{x+2}) + \dots + (\frac{1}{x+9} - \frac{1}{x+10})$
Ichki hadlar qisqarib ketadi va faqat birinchi hamda oxirgi hadlar qoladi:
$\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x+10} = \frac{x+10-x}{x(x+10)} = \frac{10}{x(x+10)}$
Tenglamaga ko'ra, bu ifoda $\frac{10}{39}$ ga teng:
$\frac{10}{x(x+10)} = \frac{10}{39}$
Maxrajlarni tenglashtiramiz:
$x(x+10) = 39$
$x^2 + 10x - 39 = 0$
Kvadrat tenglamani yechamiz:
$(x - 3)(x + 13) = 0$
$x > 0$ shartga ko'ra, musbat yechim $x = 3$.</solution>
<answer>3</answer>
</problem_5>

<problem>$p$ va $q$ tub sonlari uchun $p^2 - 2q^2 = 1$ tenglik o'rinli bo'lsa, $p^2 + q^2$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 13  B) 17  C) 25  D) 29</choices>
<solution>Tenglamani quyidagi ko'rinishga keltiramiz:
$p^2 - 1 = 2q^2 \implies (p-1)(p+1) = 2q^2$
Agar $p = 2$ bo'lsa, tenglamaning chap qismi $2^2 - 1 = 3$ bo'ladi, bu esa $2q^2$ ga teng bo'lishi mumkin emas (chunki 3 juft son emas). Demak, $p$ toq tub son.
Agar $p$ toq bo'lsa, $(p-1)$ va $(p+1)$ ikkita ketma-ket juft son bo'ladi. Ularning biri 2 ga, ikkinchisi esa albatta 4 ga karrali bo'ladi.
Demak, ularning ko'paytmasi $2 \times 4 = 8$ ga karrali. Bu shuni anglatadiki, tenglamaning chap qismi 8 ga bo'linadi.
Agar $2q^2$ soni 8 ga bo'linsa, demak, $q^2$ soni 4 ga bo'linadi, bundan kelib chiqadiki, $q$ juft son bo'lishi kerak.
Yagona juft tub son $q = 2$. 
$q = 2$ ni tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $p^2 - 2(2^2) = 1 \implies p^2 - 8 = 1 \implies p^2 = 9 \implies p = 3$.
Haqiqatan ham, 3 va 2 ikkalasi ham tub sonlar.
Bizdan $p^2 + q^2$ qiymatini topish so'ralgan: $3^2 + 2^2 = 9 + 4 = 13$.</solution>
<answer>A</answer>

<problem>Ifodaning qiymatini hisoblang: $\frac{3^{2025} - 3^{2023}}{3^{2024} + 3^{2023}}$</problem>
<choices>A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 1,5</choices>
<solution>Kasrning surat va maxrajidan eng kichik darajali ko'paytuvchini, ya'ni $3^{2023}$ ni qavsdan tashqariga chiqaramiz.
Surat: $3^{2025} - 3^{2023} = 3^{2023} \cdot (3^2 - 1) = 3^{2023} \cdot (9 - 1) = 3^{2023} \cdot 8$
Maxraj: $3^{2024} + 3^{2023} = 3^{2023} \cdot (3^1 + 1) = 3^{2023} \cdot (3 + 1) = 3^{2023} \cdot 4$
Kasrni yozamiz:
$\frac{3^{2023} \cdot 8}{3^{2023} \cdot 4}$
$3^{2023}$ lar qisqarib ketadi, va natija: $\frac{8}{4} = 2$.</solution>
