<problem>Agar $x+y=5$ va $x^2+y^2=17$ bo'lsa, $|x-y|$ ifodaning qiymatini toping.</problem>

$(x-y)^2 = x^2 - 2xy + y^2 = (x^2 + y^2) - 2xy = 17 - 8 = 9$.

$\triangle ABC$ ning barcha ichki burchaklari yig'indisi $180^\circ$ ga teng:
$\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = (x + 30^\circ) + x + x = 180^\circ$.
$3x + 30^\circ = 180^\circ \Rightarrow 3x = 150^\circ \Rightarrow x = 50^\circ$.
Demak, $\angle ABC = 50^\circ$.</solution>

<choices>A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 5</choices>
<solution>Ifoda konyunksiya (AND/$\land$) orqali bog'langan. U rost bo'lishi uchun har ikkala qism rost bo'lishi kerak: $C = 1$ va $(A \lor \neg B) = 1$.

$(A \lor \neg B)$ ifodani tekshiramiz:

- Agar $A=0, B=1$ bo'lsa: $0 \lor \neg 1 = 0 \lor 0 = 0$ (Yolg'on).
- Agar $A=1, B=0$ bo'lsa: $1 \lor \neg 0 = 1 \lor 1 = 1$ (Rost). To'plam: (1, 0, 1).
- Agar $A=1, B=1$ bo'lsa: $1 \lor \neg 1 = 1 \lor 0 = 1$ (Rost). To'plam: (1, 1, 1).
Jami 3 xil to'plamda natija rost bo'ladi.</solution>

<problem>$p$ va $q$ tub sonlar bo'lib, ular $p^2 - 2q^2 = 1$ tenglikni qanoatlantiradi. $p+q$ yig'indining qiymatini toping.</problem>
<solution>Tenglikni quyidagicha yozib olamiz: $p^2 - 1 = 2q^2 \Rightarrow (p-1)(p+1) = 2q^2$.
$p$ tub son va $p^2 = 2q^2 + 1 \ge 3$ bo'lgani uchun, $p$ toq sondir (agar $p=2$ bo'lsa, $3=2q^2$ bo'lib echim yo'q). 
$p$ toq bo'lsa, $p-1$ va $p+1$ ketma-ket keluvchi juft sonlar bo'ladi. Ketma-ket juft sonlarning biri 2 ga, ikkinchisi albatta 4 ga karrali, demak ularning ko'paytmasi 8 ga bo'linadi.
Bundan $2q^2$ ham 8 ga bo'linadi, ya'ni $q^2$ 4 ga qoldiqsiz bo'linishi kerak. 
Demak, $q$ juft son bo'lishi zarur. Yagona juft tub son bu $q=2$.
Uni tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $p^2 - 2(2^2) = 1 \Rightarrow p^2 - 8 = 1 \Rightarrow p^2 = 9 \Rightarrow p=3$ (3 ham tub son).
Shunday qilib, tub sonlar juftligi faqat $p=3$ va $q=2$. Yig'indi: $3 + 2 = 5$.</solution>
<answer>5</answer>
</example>

Ammo 3 raqami joylashgan nuqta minalashtirilgan bo'lib, robot u nuqtaga sakrab tusha olmaydi. Robotning 0 nuqtadan 7 nuqtaga yetib borishi uchun necha xil usul mavjud?</problem>
<solution>Har bir $n$ nuqtaga kelish usullari sonini $W(n)$ deb belgilaymiz. Dinamik dasturlash asosida $W(n) = W(n-1) + W(n-2)$ formulasidan foydalanamiz, faqat $W(3) = 0$ (minalangan).
$W(0) = 1$ (boshlang'ich nuqta)
$W(1) = 1$
$W(2) = W(1) + W(0) = 1 + 1 = 2$
$W(3) = 0$ (taqiqlangan)
$W(4) = W(3) + W(2) = 0 + 2 = 2$
$W(5) = W(4) + W(3) = 2 + 0 = 2$
$W(6) = W(5) + W(4) = 2 + 2 = 4$
$W(7) = W(6) + W(5) = 4 + 2 = 6$.
Jami 6 xil usul mavjud.</solution>
