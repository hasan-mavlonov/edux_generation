$S = \dfrac{2 + 46}{2} \cdot 12 = 24 \cdot 12 = 288$.
Boshlang'ich $b = 5$ bo'lgani uchun yakuniy qiymat: $b = 5 + 288 = 293$.</solution>

$x^3 + \dfrac{1}{x^3} = \left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)^3 - 3\left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right) = 4^3 - 3\cdot 4 = 64 - 12 = 52$.

3) Berilgan ifodaga qo'yamiz:
$52 - 2 \cdot 14 = 52 - 28 = 24$.</solution>

<problem>$N$ uch xonali natural sonni 7 ga bo'lganda qoldiq 4 ga, 9 ga bo'lganda qoldiq 5 ga, 11 ga bo'lganda esa qoldiq 6 ga teng bo'ladi. Ushbu shartni qanoatlantiruvchi eng kichik $N$ uch xonali sonning raqamlari yig'indisini toping.</problem>
<solution>Berilgan shartlarni qoldiqlar tenglamasi ko'rinishida yozamiz:
$N \equiv 4 \pmod 7 \implies 2N \equiv 8 \equiv 1 \pmod 7$,
$N \equiv 5 \pmod 9 \implies 2N \equiv 10 \equiv 1 \pmod 9$,
$N \equiv 6 \pmod{11} \implies 2N \equiv 12 \equiv 1 \pmod{11}$.

Demak, $2N - 1$ soni 7, 9 va 11 ga qoldiqsiz bo'linadi.
$\text{EKUK}(7, 9, 11) = 7 \cdot 9 \cdot 11 = 693$.
Shuning uchun $2N - 1 = 693k$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$).
Eng kichik musbat qiymat $k=1$ da erishiladi:
$2N - 1 = 693 \implies 2N = 694 \implies N = 347$.
$N = 347$ soni uch xonali son. Uning raqamlari yig'indisi: $3 + 4 + 7 = 14$.</solution>

Dastlabki qiymatlar:
$a_1 = 2$ ('0', '1'),
$a_2 = 3$ ('00', '01', '10'),
$a_3 = 2 + 3 = 5$,
$a_4 = 3 + 5 = 8$,
$a_5 = 5 + 8 = 13$,
$a_6 = 8 + 13 = 21$.
Demak, uzunligi 6 bo'lgan bunday kodlar soni 21 ta.</solution>
