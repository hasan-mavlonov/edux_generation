$\triangle ABC$ teng yonli ($AB = AC$), shuning uchun $\angle ABC = \angle C = 2x$.
$\triangle ABC$ burchaklari yig'indisi: $x + 2x + 2x = 180^\circ \implies 5x = 180^\circ \implies x = 36^\circ$.
U holda $\angle ABC = 2x = 72^\circ$.</solution>
