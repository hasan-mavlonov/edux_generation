<problem>$a$ va $b$ sonlari uchun $a+b=10$ va $a^2-b^2=40$ bo'lsa, $a^2+b^2$ ning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 52  B) 58  C) 64  D) 100</choices>
<solution>Qisqa ko'paytirish formulasiga ko'ra $a^2-b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$. Qiymatlarni o'rniga qo'yamiz: $40 = (a-b) \cdot 10$, bundan $a-b = 4$. Bizda tenglamalar sistemasi hosil bo'ldi: $a+b=10$ va $a-b=4$. Ularni qo'shsak: $2a = 14 \implies a = 7$. Ayirsak: $2b = 6 \implies b = 3$. Endi $a^2+b^2$ ni hisoblaymiz: $7^2 + 3^2 = 49 + 9 = 58$.</solution>

<problem>$N = \overline{4a7b2}$ ko'rinishidagi besh xonali son 36 ga qoldiqsiz bo'linadi. $a+b$ yig'indining eng katta qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 13  B) 14  C) 15  D) 17</choices>
<solution>Son 36 ga qoldiqsiz bo'linishi uchun u ham 4 ga, ham 9 ga bo'linishi kerak. 
1) 4 ga bo'linish belgisi: sonning oxirgi ikkita raqamidan tashkil topgan son 4 ga bo'linishi kerak. Ya'ni $\overline{b2}$ soni 4 ga bo'linishi kerak. Bu faqat $b \in \{1, 3, 5, 7, 9\}$ bo'lganda o'rinli (12, 32, 52, 72, 92).
2) 9 ga bo'linish belgisi: sonning raqamlari yig'indisi 9 ga bo'linishi kerak. Raqamlar yig'indisi: $4 + a + 7 + b + 2 = a + b + 13$. 
$a+b+13$ yig'indi 9 ga bo'linishi uchun u 18 yoki 27 bo'lishi mumkin (chunki eng katta raqamlar $a=9, b=9$ bo'lganda umumiy yig'indi 31 gacha boradi). 
Agar $a+b+13 = 18$ bo'lsa, $a+b = 5$.
Agar $a+b+13 = 27$ bo'lsa, $a+b = 14$.
Eng katta qiymat 14. Bu holat $b \in \{5, 7, 9\}$ va $a \in \{9, 7, 5\}$ bo'lganda amalga oshadi.</solution>

<problem>$ABC$ uchburchakda $\angle C = 120^\circ$. $AB$ tomonda $D$ va $E$ nuqtalar shunday olinganki, $D$ nuqta $A$ va $E$ nuqtalar orasida joylashgan. Agar $AC = AE$ va $BC = BD$ bo'lsa, $\angle DCE$ burchakni toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 15°  B) 40°  C) 30°  D) 45°</choices>
<solution>$AC = AE$ bo'lgani uchun $\triangle ACE$ teng yonli. Uning asosidagi burchaklari $\angle ACE = \angle AEC = \frac{180^\circ - \angle A}{2} = 90^\circ - \frac{\angle A}{2}$.
Xuddi shunday, $BC = BD$ bo'lgani uchun $\triangle BCD$ ham teng yonli va $\angle BCD = \angle BDC = 90^\circ - \frac{\angle B}{2}$.
Ushbu ikki burchak yig'indisi: $\angle ACE + \angle BCD = 180^\circ - \frac{\angle A + \angle B}{2}$.
Uchburchakning ichki burchaklari yig'indisidan $\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ - \angle C = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ$.
Shuning uchun $\angle ACE + \angle BCD = 180^\circ - \frac{60^\circ}{2} = 150^\circ$.
Chizmada $D$ nuqta $A$ va $E$ orasida bo'lgani uchun, $\angle ACE = \angle ACD + \angle DCE$ va $\angle BCD = \angle BCE + \angle DCE$.
Ularni qo'shsak: $\angle ACE + \angle BCD = (\angle ACD + \angle DCE + \angle BCE) + \angle DCE = \angle C + \angle DCE$.
Bundan $150^\circ = 120^\circ + \angle DCE \implies \angle DCE = 30^\circ$.</solution>
<answer>C</answer>

<problem>Quyidagi algoritm ishlashi natijasida S o'zgaruvchisi qanday qiymat qabul qiladi?

S = 0
N = 1
Toki N <= 5 bo'lsa, takrorla:
    Agar N % 2 == 1 bo'lsa:
        S = S + N * 2
    Aks holda:
        S = S - N
    N = N + 1

Izoh: % belgisi qoldiqni hisoblash amali.</problem>
<choices>A) 10  B) 15  C) 8  D) 12</choices>
<solution>Algoritm N=1 dan 5 gacha aylanadi (tsikl):

3-qadam: N=3 (toq). S = 0 + 3 * 2 = 6. N=4 bo'ladi.
4-qadam: N=4 (juft). S = 6 - 4 = 2. N=5 bo'ladi.
5-qadam: N=5 (toq). S = 2 + 5 * 2 = 12. N=6 bo'ladi.
N=6 bo'lganda tsikl sharti (N <= 5) bajarilmaydi va to'xtaydi. Yakuniy natija S = 12.</solution>

<problem>$a$ va $b$ musbat haqiqiy sonlar bo'lib, ular $5a^2 + b^2 - 4ab - 12a + 36 = 0$ tenglikni qanoatlantiradi. Agar $x = \frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a}$ bo'lsa, $x$ ning qiymatini toping.</problem>
<choices>A) 2  B) 2.5  C) 3  D) 1.5</choices>
<solution>Berilgan tenglamani to'la kvadratlarga ajratamiz:
$5a^2 + b^2 - 4ab - 12a + 36 = 0$
$(4a^2 - 4ab + b^2) + (a^2 - 12a + 36) = 0$
Birinchi qavs $(2a - b)^2$ ga, ikkinchi qavs $(a - 6)^2$ ga teng.
$(2a - b)^2 + (a - 6)^2 = 0$.
Ikkita musbat (yoki nolga teng) sonlar yig'indisi faqatgina ikkisi ham nolga teng bo'lgandagina nol bo'ladi:
$a - 6 = 0 \implies a = 6$
$2a - b = 0 \implies b = 2a = 12$.
Endi so'ralgan ifodani hisoblaymiz:
$x = \frac{6}{12} + \frac{12}{6} = 0.5 + 2 = 2.5$.</solution>
